English Basics — Part 2

Clothes, food, and the home

This is the second part of the course. It moves from grammar basics into everyday topics: what you wear, what you eat, and the rooms of a house — plus one important grammar point, the difference between I wear and I am wearing.

What to focus on: Vocabulary units (clothes, food, meals, house, living room, bathroom) are about recall — can you name the thing without thinking? The grammar units (wear vs wearing, plurals) are about noticing the pattern and applying it to a new word, not just memorising the examples.
